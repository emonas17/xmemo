Connecting NMEA3WiFi

Connect your phone and the NMEA3WiFi box to the same boat router. Xmarine is pre-filled with box IP 192.168.1.111, TCP port 10110 and UDP port 10111. Set matching values in the box, or enter the box's actual address and ports in Xmarine. Start with TCP. In the box, enable P1/P2>TCP for wired NMEA data, ST>TCP for SeaTalk data and TCP>ST for pilot commands. The app's Network settings contain a fuller guide.

Autopilot safety

When you enable PILOT and send a command, you remain fully responsible for vessel control, navigation, keeping watch and all consequences of that command. Stay at the helm and be ready to intervene immediately. Xmarine does not replace your vessel's primary controls.
