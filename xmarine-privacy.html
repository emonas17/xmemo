Xmarine Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Xmarine displays marine data from equipment you connect and can send autopilot commands to a gateway you configure. It does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics.

Information on your device

Connection settings, display preferences and saved routes are stored on your device. Xmarine may use your device location to show your position when boat GPS data is unavailable. Marine data received from your boat, including position and heading, is used to update the display. Xmarine does not upload these settings, routes or marine data to a server operated by us.

Network connections

Xmarine connects to the local or remote marine gateway address you enter. Pilot commands are sent only when you use the pilot controls. If you use map layers or weather forecasts, your device connects to map and forecast providers, including Apple Maps, OpenStreetMap, OpenSeaMap, EMODnet and Open-Meteo. Those providers may receive your IP address and the map area or forecast coordinates requested. Their own privacy policies apply to their services.

Permissions and choices

Local Network access is used to communicate with boat equipment. Location access is used for position display when you allow it. You can change these permissions in iOS Settings, stop the network connection in Xmarine, and remove locally stored app data by deleting the app. We do not sell personal data or track you across apps and websites.
